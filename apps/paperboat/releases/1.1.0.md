# Paperboat 1.1.0

Image tag: `1.1.0`. Schema version: 2 (was 1).

## Summary

Fish aging: each fish gets a condition score, and fish past the age limit of their species
retire from the rentable stock.

## Changes

- Each species has a retirement age in days (`retire_after_days`).
- Each fish has a condition score from 0 to 100. The score is 100 for a new fish and 30 at
  the retirement age of its species.
- The aging job of the worker (every 30 seconds) updates the scores and retires fish that
  are older than the limit. Only a fish that is in a tank retires. A fish that is with a
  customer retires after it comes back.
- `GET /api/inventory` adds `condition` and `retire_after_days` to each fish.
- The inventory board shows a condition meter for each fish and counts retired fish.
- Health certificates show the condition score.

## Migration

- Migration 2 adds `species.retire_after_days` (default 730) and `fish.condition`
  (default 100). It sets the retirement age of the eight stock species and computes the
  condition of each fish from its age.
- The migration only adds columns. Release 1.0.0 continues to work on schema version 2, so
  the 1.0.0 pods keep serving while the hook runs and during the rolling update.
- The migration runs as a Helm `pre-upgrade` hook, in one transaction. It is forward-only.
  There is no down migration.

## Risk

Medium. This release changes the schema and takes fish out of the rentable stock.

- Expect some fish to change to `retired` in the first minute after the upgrade. With the
  starting stock, five fish are older than the limit of their species. The count of
  available fish goes down by the same number as these fish come back to their tanks.
- A retired fish does not return to `available`. A rollback does not undo retirements.
- Rollback to 1.0.0 needs no schema change. The new columns stay in the database and 1.0.0
  does not read them.
- Watch after rollout: `fish_available` in `/api/stats`, and the `aging` lines in the worker
  log.
